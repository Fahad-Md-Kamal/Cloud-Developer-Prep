# Chapter 20: Observability, Monitoring, and On-Call Readiness

Master production observability and incident response. Learn to build comprehensive monitoring systems, handle on-call responsibilities, and maintain high system reliability.

## Learning Objectives

- Build comprehensive observability strategies
- Implement effective alerting and incident response
- Master on-call procedures and escalation
- Design SLIs, SLOs, and error budgets
- Handle post-incident analysis and improvement

## Key Topics

### 1. Observability Strategy
- Three pillars: logs, metrics, and traces
- Monitoring pyramid and telemetry data
- Service Level Indicators (SLIs) and Objectives (SLOs)
- Error budgets and reliability targets
- Observability vs monitoring distinctions

### 2. Alerting and Incident Response
- Alert design and noise reduction
- Incident classification and severity levels
- On-call rotation and escalation procedures
- Incident command and communication
- War room coordination and decision making

### 3. Production Readiness
- Pre-deployment checklists and validations
- Runbook creation and maintenance
- Disaster recovery planning and testing
- Capacity planning and resource management
- Performance baseline establishment

### 4. Continuous Improvement
- Post-incident review processes
- Blameless post-mortems and learning
- System reliability improvement tracking
- Automation opportunities identification
- Knowledge sharing and documentation

## Practical Examples

- Setting up comprehensive monitoring for microservices
- Building effective alerting and on-call procedures
- Conducting post-incident reviews and improvements

## Interview Preparation

- Observability strategy design questions
- Incident response and leadership scenarios
- System reliability and improvement discussions
