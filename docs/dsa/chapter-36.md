# Chapter 36: Mastering Data Structures & Algorithms with Blind 75, Grind 75 & NeetCode 150

Acing the coding interview is a non-negotiable step for landing roles at top-tier companies like Lawstronaut and Optimizely. This chapter provides a focused, pattern-driven approach to mastering three curated problem lists—"Blind 75," "Grind 75," and the expanded "NeetCode 150"—that contain essential LeetCode problems frequently appearing in technical interviews. Instead of memorizing solutions, you will learn to recognize underlying patterns, enabling you to solve not just these problems, but hundreds of similar ones.

**What's Covered:**
- **Blind 75:** The original foundational 75 problems that every candidate should know
- **Grind 75:** A modernized, customizable alternative with improved problem selection and scheduling
- **NeetCode 150:** An expanded set of 150 problems (includes Blind 75 + 75 additional problems) covering more advanced patterns and edge cases

## Learning Objectives

-   Identify common data structure and algorithm patterns (e.g., Two Pointers, Sliding Window, BFS/DFS).
-   Apply these patterns to solve the 75 essential problems and their variations.
-   Develop a systematic problem-solving framework for coding interviews.
-   Analyze time and space complexity with confidence.
-   Write clean, efficient, and production-quality Python code under pressure.

## 1. Understanding the Problem Lists: Blind 75, Grind 75 & NeetCode 150

### 1.1 Blind 75: The Original Classic

Created around 2018 by a Facebook engineer on the Blind platform, this list of 75 problems became the gold standard for interview preparation. It covers all essential patterns and data structures with minimal overlap.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Comprehensive coverage of fundamental patterns
- Focus on high-frequency interview problems
- Battle-tested across thousands of successful candidates
- Good balance between difficulty levels

### 1.2 Grind 75: The Modern Alternative

Developed by the founder of Grind (now Techquired), Grind 75 is a modernized replacement for Blind 75 with several improvements:

**Key Features:**
- **~67% overlap** with Blind 75, but includes updated/different problems
- **Customizable study plans** (1-8 weeks based on your timeline)
- **Structured difficulty progression** from easy to hard
- **Built-in progress tracking** at [grind75.com](https://grind75.com)
- **Frequency-based ordering** prioritizing most commonly asked problems
- **Time estimates** for each problem to help plan study sessions

**When to Choose Grind 75:**
- You have limited time (2-4 weeks) and need a structured schedule
- You want built-in progress tracking and scheduling
- You prefer a more modern problem selection
- You're preparing for interviews at companies that follow recent trends

### 1.3 NeetCode 150: The Comprehensive Collection

NeetCode 150 extends Blind 75 with 75 additional problems, providing deeper coverage of advanced patterns and edge cases. It includes video explanations and is organized by pattern for systematic learning.

### 1.4 Pattern Recognition Over Rote Memorization

The power of these lists isn't the specific problems themselves, but the patterns they represent. Companies test your problem-solving ability, not your memory. By focusing on patterns, you build a reusable toolkit.

**Why Patterns Matter:**
-   **Scalability:** Learning one pattern (e.g., Sliding Window) unlocks dozens of problems.
-   **Adaptability:** You can adapt patterns to solve unfamiliar problems.
-   **Communication:** You can articulate your approach clearly to the interviewer ("This looks like a Two Pointers problem because...").

**Which List Should You Use?**
- **Short timeline (1-4 weeks):** Grind 75 for its structured schedule
- **Traditional approach:** Blind 75 for the classic preparation path
- **Comprehensive preparation (6-12 weeks):** NeetCode 150 or Blind 75 + Grind 75 combined
- **Maximum coverage:** All three lists, focusing on pattern mastery

### 2.3 Study Strategy: Combining All Three Lists

For maximum preparation efficiency:

1. **Weeks 1-2:** Start with Grind 75's Week 1 problems (easy patterns)
2. **Weeks 3-4:** Complete core Blind 75 problems (medium patterns)
3. **Weeks 5-8:** Work through NeetCode 150 additional problems (advanced patterns)
4. **Weeks 9+:** Review and solve variations from all lists

**Pattern Overlap Benefits:**
- Problems from different lists often use the same patterns
- Solving the same pattern in different contexts reinforces understanding
- You'll recognize patterns faster in actual interviews
