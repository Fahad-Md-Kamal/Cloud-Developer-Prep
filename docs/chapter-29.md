# Chapter 29: Leadership for Engineers — Mentoring, Reviews, and Team Scaling

Develop technical leadership skills essential for senior and staff engineer roles. Learn to mentor team members, conduct effective reviews, and scale engineering teams successfully.

## Learning Objectives

- Master technical mentoring and coaching techniques
- Conduct effective code reviews and technical interviews
- Scale engineering teams and establish best practices
- Handle difficult conversations and conflict resolution
- Build inclusive and high-performing technical teams

## Key Topics

### 1. Technical Mentoring
- Identifying mentoring opportunities and needs
- Effective code review and feedback techniques
- Knowledge transfer and documentation strategies
- Career development planning for engineers
- Building psychological safety in technical discussions

### 2. Performance Management
- Setting clear technical expectations and goals
- Performance review processes for engineers
- Handling underperformance and improvement plans
- Recognition and promotion advocacy
- 360-degree feedback and peer reviews

### 3. Team Scaling Strategies
- Hiring processes and technical interviewing
- Onboarding new engineers effectively
- Establishing team culture and practices
- Technical decision-making processes
- Managing technical debt and architecture evolution

### 4. Leadership Communication
- Technical presentations and architecture reviews
- Stakeholder communication and expectation management
- Cross-team collaboration and alignment
- Handling conflicts and disagreements
- Building consensus on technical decisions

## Practical Examples

- Designing effective mentoring programs
- Creating technical interview processes
- Building team culture and practices

## Interview Preparation

- Leadership scenario-based questions
- Team management and scaling challenges
- Technical decision-making under pressure
